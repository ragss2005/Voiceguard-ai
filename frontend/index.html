Dataset Download & Setup Guide

The ASVspoof 2019 LA dataset is hosted by the University of Edinburgh DataShare archive:
https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/items/31074a11-b6f6-4e92-a4ad-07093f8c0c45 ↗

  1. Download the official archive LA.zip (~25 GB compressed).
  2. Extract into backend/data/ASVspoof2019_LA or an external drive.
  3. Ensure the protocols folder containing ASVspoof2019.LA.cm.train.trn.txt and ASVspoof2019.LA.cm.dev.trl.txt is present.
  4. Click Scan Directory ↻ above to verify partition indices.
  5. Head to Model Management to trigger the training benchmark.
